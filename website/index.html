Why Oko

Three pillars of digital sovereignty

🌍

100% On-Device RAG

Retrieval-Augmented Generation that never phones home. Your documents, your context, your rules — processed entirely on your hardware. Zero cloud dependency. Zero data leakage.

👁

Sub-Second Vision

Drop a screenshot, receipt, or handwritten note — Oko parses it instantly with on-device multimodal models. No upload. No server-side OCR. Just fast, private understanding.

⭐

SQLite Infinite Memory

Every conversation, every context shift — stored locally in SQLite. Oko builds a persistent knowledge graph of your workflow across sessions. No subscriptions, no data caps.
